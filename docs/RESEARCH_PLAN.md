# Research roadmap

This independent workspace supports reproducibility and evidence organization. The implemented DC solver is a baseline educational model, not a novel power system or a validated high-voltage prototype.

| Stage | Deliverable | Acceptance evidence | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow foundation | Records, DC solver, app, criteria reporting and evidence bundles | Automated tests and web/Python parity | Implemented |
| Differentiated concept | Specific hypothesis and related-work/program comparison | Independent novelty and nonduplication review | Not established |
| Architecture grounding | Appropriate AC/DC/converter, protection, insulation, thermal and stability models | Versioned assumptions and independent engineering review | Not implemented |
| Reliability assessment | Failure modes, dependencies, redundancy and availability assumptions | Data-backed reliability model and review | Notes only |
| Experimental validation | Authorized calibrated test data, controls and uncertainty | Raw evidence, repeatability and qualified review | No real data or equipment connection |
| Prototype/program evaluation | Application criteria and applicable engagement scope | Independent validation and current notice review | No submission |

Next: define a real research question and architecture boundary, identify qualified engineering/experimental collaborators, select appropriate validated modeling tools and collect authorized evidence. Treat the example as software demonstration only. The repository does not configure physical equipment or replace detailed engineering assessment.
