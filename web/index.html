METHOD & CONTEXT

Show the boundary of the model.

Radial DC constant-power flow

A damped backward/forward sweep solves positive node voltages from a stiff source. Current summation, voltage residuals and total energy balance are checked. This is not an AC grid or dynamic converter model.

Convergence is not approval

Failed solves withhold metrics and criteria assessments. Converged results are compared only to owner-declared current, voltage and source-power criteria. No equipment-rating, reliability or safety approval is inferred.

Independent research workspace

No insulation coordination, grounding, protection, fault/arc analysis, thermal limit, switching transient, stability, availability or hardware integration is implemented. Qualified independent engineering models and experimental validation remain separate work.
